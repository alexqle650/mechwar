Mech Wars: Crate Clash

You are a little robot pilot. First build your mech at the Mech Workshop (follow the cyan arrow): buy parts off the conveyor belt, install one in every slot, then hit Build. Walk up to it and press R to climb in. Put spare parts on display pads to earn scrap. Then stomp north and fight the guardian bots around each crate, then hold E to crack it open. Fight the wave that comes after for bonus loot, or flee with the normal haul. Equip parts in the Mech Bay to raise your Power and open the gates to bigger zones with better crates. Night lasts 1 minute every 10: better crates, angrier bots. Stop Rival Mechs before they steal your crate. Every zone has a boss in its arena, and you always have 3 missions for extra scrap.
