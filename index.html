Mech Wars: Crate Clash

  1. Build a mech. Buy parts off the conveyor (E), fill all 6 slots at the Mech Workshop (B), then climb in (R).
  2. Crack crates. Head north, beat the guard bots, hold E to open. Fight the wave after for bonus loot, or flee with it.
  3. Get stronger. Better parts raise your Power and open gates to tougher zones with rarer crates and a boss in each.
  4. Show off. Spare parts on display pads earn scrap. Play with a friend (P) to ambush, steal and team up.
